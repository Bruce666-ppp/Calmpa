Simulation boundaries

The simulation follows the documented direction of movement: petals close gradually while desk presence continues, then reopen and reset when the person leaves. Its progress control is unitless, and the play sequence is accelerated for explanation. Timing, motion geometry and interpolation are illustrative; the report does not supply firmware, calibrated servo angles, distance thresholds or a prescribed break duration.

Illumination is visible in the making photographs and described in the poster. The materials do not define a consistent colour-to-state mapping or a complete light-control specification. The simulation therefore explains petal motion only.

Interpretation and authorship

Desk proximity is used as a proxy for presence, not a direct measurement of sitting posture, fatigue, emotion or health. Claims about reduction in sitting time, productivity gains, medical benefit or validated long-term behaviour change are not made. The numerical claims printed on poster sheet 1 are not reproduced as project findings.

The contribution list summarises the work documented within an individual thesis. It does not assert that every electronic component, mechanical part or line of firmware was developed without assistance. More granular assistance credits can be added when confirmed by the author.
